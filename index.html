    MNVR Product Documentation

    MNVR (Mobile Network Video Recorder) is an in-vehicle device for heavy vehicles such as buses. It records camera video for surveillance, tracks the vehicle's location, monitors vehicle health over the CAN bus, keeps a two-way TCP connection with a backend server, and supports voice calls.
